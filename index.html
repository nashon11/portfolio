Design + Engineering

I sit between design and engineering. Because I write the code, I know what happens after a design leaves Figma: layouts have to survive real data and small screens, and every state has to exist somewhere in the code. I try to think about that earlier, while I'm still designing. I'm newer to design than to engineering, and I'm building that side project by project.

  1. IdeaWhat is it for, and who will use it?
  2. User flowThe steps someone takes, and where they could get stuck.
  3. InterfaceScreens and states in Figma, Lunacy or Penpot.
  4. ImplementationReact, Next.js, JavaScript, FastAPI, Node.js, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis.
  5. TestingIntegration testing, with Git/GitHub, Docker, CI/CD and a little AWS to get it out.

About me

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in Nairobi. I started freelancing in December 2023 and, until October 2024, built ChoiceMate and a finance management app with role-based authentication and responsive UI designs in Lunacy and Penpot. After that I completed a Graduate Diploma in Fullstack Software Engineering at Moringa School / Flatiron School and a Data Structures & Algorithms course at Zindua School.

In 2025 I did a tech ops attachment at Zindua, where I maintained their LMS and worked on authentication, dashboard features and integration testing. I then finished their DevOps Engineering course in August 2025, covering CI/CD, Docker and Kubernetes.

Design is the part I'm growing into. I use Figma, Lunacy and Penpot, and I want to keep getting better at product and UI work while I keep building.
